<p class="en">
We propose TriPer, an active perception system that maintains a persistent scene state modeling exploration,
high-fidelity geometry, and open-vocabulary semantics from streaming RGB-D observations, enabling efficient
target-object grasping in cluttered and occluded scenes.
</p>

<p class="zh">
我们提出 TriPer，一种主动感知系统：从流式 RGB-D 观测中维护一个持续的场景状态，同时建模探索状态、高精度几何与开放词汇语义，
从而在杂乱、遮挡的桌面场景中高效完成目标物体抓取。
</p>
