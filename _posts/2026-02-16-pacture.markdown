<p class="en">
We propose a method to efficiently generate physically-based rendering (PBR) textures on a packed multi-view grid 
using a visual autoregressive model as multi-view generation backbone.
</p>

<p class="zh">
提出以视觉自回归模型作为多视角生成骨干，在打包的多视角网格上高效生成基于物理渲染（PBR）的材质贴图。
</p>
