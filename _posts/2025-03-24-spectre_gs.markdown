<p class="en">
We propose a 3D reconstruction method which models highly specular surfaces that reflect nearby objects through 
ray tracing in 3D Gaussian Splatting.
</p>

<p class="zh">
提出一种三维重建方法，通过在三维高斯泼溅中追踪光线，对反射邻近物体的高反光表面进行建模。
</p>
