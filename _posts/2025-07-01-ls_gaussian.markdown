<p class="en">
We propose an algorithm/hardware co-design framework for lightweight streaming 3D rendering which 
achieves 5.41× speedup over the edge GPU baseline on average and up to 17.3× speedup with the customized
accelerator.
</p>

<p class="zh">
提出一套面向轻量级流式三维渲染的算法/硬件协同设计框架，相比边缘 GPU 基线平均加速 5.41×，配合定制加速器最高可达 17.3× 加速。
</p>
