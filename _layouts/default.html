Fan Fei (费凡)费凡 (Fan Fei)

I am a fifth-year Ph.D. candidate at the Camera Intelligence Research Lab at Peking University, where I work on 3D computer vision and embodied intelligence. I am advised by Prof. Boxin Shi.

我是北京大学相机智能实验室的五年级博士生， 主要研究三维计算机视觉与具身智能，师从施柏鑫教授。

I received my B.S. (summa cum laude) in Intelligence Science and Technology from the Turing Class at the School of EECS, Peking University in 2022.

我于 2022 年在北京大学信息科学技术学院（School of EECS）图灵班 获得智能科学与技术专业荣誉学士学位。

I interned at Light Illusions under the supervision of Prof. Ping Tan from October 2023 to September 2024, and at the PKU-AI² Robotics Joint Lab of Embodied AI from June 2025 to May 2026, where I worked closely with Jiaming Liu and other researchers.

2023 年 10 月至 2024 年 9 月，我在 光影焕像 实习， 导师为谭平教授； 2025 年 6 月至 2026 年 5 月，我在北京大学-智平方具身智能联合实验室实习， 期间与刘家铭及其他研究者密切合作。

Since June 2026, I have been working at Tencent Hunyuan 3D as a research intern on PBR texture generation.

自 2026 年 6 月起，我在腾讯混元 3D 担任研究实习生， 从事 PBR 纹理生成方向的研究。

Research研究方向

My research interests: 3D computer vision, computer graphics, and embodied intelligence, including inverse rendering, 3D reconstruction, texture generation, active perception, and end-to-end policy learning.

我的研究方向为：三维计算机视觉、计算机图形学与具身智能， 包括逆向渲染、三维重建、纹理生成、主动感知与端到端策略学习。
